⚡ Sparky’s 1.5V Circuit Lab 💡

Click the switches to direct the tiny 1.5V current to the bulb!

Bulb State: OFF Circuit is open.

🛠️ How the circuit works:

  • Common Ground: The base of the bulb connects straight back to the (-) battery terminal along the bottom blue wire.
  • Series Connection: S1 and S5 are in series with the whole system—both must allow current through!
  • Parallel Paths: Current can flow through either Path A (both S2 AND S3 closed) OR Path B (just S4 closed).
  • Watch out: Don't close the ⚠️ Short Circuit switch when power reaches it, or you'll trip the battery!
